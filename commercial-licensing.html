Commercial Licensing

What you can do with the assets Dreviq Studio delivers.

Ownership on delivery

Once a produced asset (image, video, or brand-identity file) is delivered and invoiced under your plan, you own full commercial usage rights to that specific asset for your brand's marketing, advertising, and commerce use — paid social, organic social, your website, print, packaging, and marketplace listings.

What's included

  • Unlimited use of delivered assets across your own paid and organic channels
  • No per-impression, per-view, or per-platform royalties
  • Right to edit, crop, and re-cut delivered video/image assets for different formats

What's not included

  • Reselling raw, unedited Dreviq output as a standalone product or stock asset
  • Sublicensing delivered assets to an unrelated third-party brand
  • Claiming sole authorship of the underlying AI models/pipeline itself — the engine and process remain Dreviq Studio's own IP

Synthetic talent & likeness

Synthetic UGC spokespeople used in your campaigns are AI-generated, not real people, and are provided under the same commercial license as any other delivered asset for your brand's use.

Questions

Licensing terms can be adjusted for Enterprise OS plans (e.g. exclusivity, sublicensing for a client-of-a-client setup) — reach out at dreviqstudio@gmail.com to discuss.
