Data Privacy SOP

What we collect, why, and how it's handled.

What we collect

When you use this site, we collect only what you give us directly:

  • Discovery Call form — brand name, business vertical, creative needs, timeline, preferred channel, business email, and WhatsApp number
  • Shilpi (chat assistant) — the conversation itself, and any contact details you choose to share in it
  • Reference/brand assets you upload for a project we're producing for you

We do not use tracking cookies, ad pixels, or third-party analytics scripts on this site.

Why we collect it

Solely to respond to your inquiry, scope your project, and deliver the work you asked for. Nothing here is sold or shared with third parties for marketing purposes.

Where it's stored

Submissions are stored on Vercel's infrastructure (Vercel Blob storage), access-restricted to the Dreviq Studio team via a password-protected admin panel. Shilpi's conversations are processed by Anthropic's Claude API to generate responses.

How long we keep it

Inquiry and conversation records are kept while we're actively following up, and for reasonable record-keeping afterward. Reach out any time and we'll delete your data on request.

Your rights

You can ask us what we hold about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it, at any time — email dreviqstudio@gmail.com.
